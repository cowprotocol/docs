# TWAP orders
 
Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP) orders are an advanced feature of CoW Protocol. Instead of executing a trade all at once, a TWAP splits it into smaller parts and executes them at regular intervals over a time period you choose. TWAP orders work with both **EOA wallets** (like MetaMask or Rabby) and **Safe wallets**.
 
## Introduction
 
A TWAP order takes one trade and turns it into a series of smaller ones, placed at even intervals. Because each part is only a slice of the total, it has less impact on the market price, and the market gets time to recover between parts. Averaging across several points in time can also smooth out the price you get.
 
People use TWAPs in a few different ways:
 
- **Breaking up a trade** to reduce price impact, especially in pools with limited liquidity.
- **Getting an average price over time** instead of committing to a single moment.
- **Scheduling buys or sells at regular intervals**, like a recurring purchase.
### Benefits
 
- **Reduce price impact**: Every trade moves the price a little, and in shallow pools that effect adds up quickly. For example, buying `$1M` of ETH over 3 hours in 6 parts means the TWAP places an order of about `$166k` every `30min`, so the market can recover between parts. The same logic applies at any scale: splitting a `$2k` trade in a thin pool into a few parts can help too.
- **Average price over time**: Spreading a trade across a selected period means your execution isn't tied to a single price point.
- **Set it and (mostly) forget it**: Sign once, and CoW Protocol places each part for you at the interval you chose.
- **Minimum order size**: `$1k` on Mainnet, or `$1` on Gnosis Chain, Arbitrum One and Base.
## Placing a TWAP Order
 
### Connect your wallet
 
TWAP orders on _CoW Swap_ work with two kinds of wallet:
 
* 🦊 **EOA wallets** (MetaMask, Rabby, and friends): connect as usual, no special setup required.
* 🔐 **Safe wallets**: open [CoW Swap (Safe App)](https://app.safe.global/share/safe-app?appUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fswap.cow.fi&chain=eth) from your Safe. Don't have one yet? You can [create a Safe](https://app.safe.global/welcome).
Once CoW Swap is loaded and connected, you can access TWAP orders via the "TWAP" tab near the center module or the drop-down menu next to the CoW Swap logo.

### Specify parameters
 
This is a _critical_ step where you'll define how your order will be executed over time. Here's how to fine-tune your TWAP order settings:
 
<Image img={require('/img/cowswap/TWAP_params.png')} alt="Order parameters" style={{ maxWidth: '470px' }} />
 
1. **Price Protection**: This is a measure to prevent your order from being executed at a price that's too far from the market price when the order was placed. Set the percentage of price protection to define the acceptable price range for your trades. For instance, a 10% price protection means that your order will not execute if the price moves unfavorably by more than 10% from the price at the time the order was made.
2. **Number of Parts**: Decide how many discrete parts you want to divide your order into. This choice will determine the granularity of your TWAP strategy. For example, if you choose to split your order into 2 parts and you are selling a total of 2 WETH, each part will involve selling 1 WETH. {/* TODO: confirm per-part minimums with the technical team. Current docs say $5k on Mainnet / $5 on Gnosis Chain, which doesn't line up with the $1k / $1 order minimums above. */}Each part is executed on-chain, meaning there's a cost associated with each execution, so very small parts may not be worthwhile.
3. **Total Duration**: Choose the total time frame over which your order should be executed. The TWAP strategy will distribute your trades evenly across this duration. Select from predefined durations like 1 hour, or set a custom duration based on your trading strategy.
4. **Part Duration** _(read-only)_: View the duration of each individual part of your order. It is calculated automatically as the total duration divided by the number of parts. For instance, if your total duration is 1 hour and you have 2 parts, each part will have a duration of 30 minutes.
5. **Sell per Part** _(read-only)_: After setting the number of parts and the total duration, the interface will display the sell amount for each part. Ensure this aligns with your trade expectations.
6. **Buy per Part** _(read-only)_: Similarly, for a buy TWAP order, this will indicate how much of the token you are looking to purchase in each part of the order.
Carefully review and adjust your order parameters to align with market conditions, anticipated movements, and your trading objectives, as they are crucial in dictating the execution and efficacy of your TWAP strategy. Once you have tailored these details to your satisfaction, you can move on to the next step to finalize and place your order.

### Review and confirm
 
Check all the details of your order, including price protection and limit price. Once you're happy, confirm in your wallet.
 
**With an EOA wallet**, you sign a single transaction to create the TWAP order. Depending on the token and your existing allowances, you may also need one of the following before that:
 
* a **permit signature** (for tokens that support permits), or
* an **on-chain approval transaction** (for tokens that don't, or where your current allowance isn't enough).
Your wallet will prompt you for whichever applies, so there's nothing to configure.
 
**With a Safe wallet**, sign the transaction in your Safe interface.

## Cancelling a TWAP order
 
Cancelling a TWAP order takes a few simple steps. Begin by locating your active TWAP order in the "Your Orders" section.
 
<Image img={require('/img/cowswap/TWAP_orderMenu.png')} alt="Order menu" />
 
Find the order you intend to cancel and click the three-dot menu icon at the end of the order's row. Select "Cancel order" from the dropdown menu to start the cancellation process.
 
<Image img={require('/img/cowswap/TWAP_cancelOrder.png')} alt="Cancel order" style={{ maxWidth: '470px' }} />
 
A confirmation window will appear, summarizing the order you are about to cancel, including the swap details and the transaction type. This is your chance to double-check that you're cancelling the right order.
 
When you confirm by clicking "Request cancellation", a cancellation transaction is created.
 
* **EOA wallets**: confirm the transaction in your wallet. Cancellation happens on-chain, so it costs gas.
* **Safe wallets**: the transaction appears in your Safe and isn't final until all required owners have signed. Cancellation is also on-chain and costs gas. It's worth coordinating with your co-signers promptly, especially if you need to cancel quickly because market conditions have changed.
Once the cancellation transaction is confirmed, the order is removed from your active orders list and considered cancelled.
 
## Conclusion
 
TWAP orders spread a trade across time, which can reduce price impact and give you a smoother average price, whatever wallet you use. Try it out and let us know your experience at [info@cow.fi](mailto:info@cow.fi)!
